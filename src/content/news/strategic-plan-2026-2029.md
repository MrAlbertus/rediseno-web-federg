After a year of consultation with member associations, the federation has agreed a new strategic plan for 2026–2029.

## Three priorities

1. **Earlier and fairer diagnosis.** Too many patients wait years for a diagnosis. We will push for better access to genetic testing across Europe.
2. **Patients in research.** Patients should help shape research questions, not only take part in trials.
3. **Stronger member associations.** Shared tools, training and a common voice.

## What happens next

Each priority will have a working group open to all members. The first meetings take place online before the end of the year.
