## What we do

We bring together patient associations from across Europe so that people living with rare and genetic kidney diseases are heard where decisions about their care, research and rights are made.

Our work rests on four pillars:

- **Advocacy** – raising the needs of patients with European institutions, health authorities and policy makers.
- **Representation** – making sure patients have a seat at the table in research networks, scientific meetings and expert groups.
- **Engagement** – helping member associations share knowledge, resources and good practice.
- **Collaboration** – working alongside clinicians, researchers and other patient organisations.

## Our mission

To improve the health and quality of life of all those affected by rare and/or genetic kidney diseases, through advocacy, representation, engagement and collaboration.

## Why our name matters

*FEDERG* comes from the French *Fédération des Associations Européennes de Patients atteints d'une maladie rénale rare ou d'origine génétique*. The name reminds us of where we started and of the people we serve: patients and families who too often feel alone with a diagnosis few people have heard of.

## Strategic plan 2026–2029

Our strategic plan sets three priorities for the coming years:

1. **Earlier and fairer diagnosis** – better access to genetic testing and specialist centres in every European country.
2. **Patients in research** – involving patients from the very start of clinical studies and registries.
3. **Stronger member associations** – training, shared tools and a louder common voice.
