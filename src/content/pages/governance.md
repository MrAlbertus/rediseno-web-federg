## General Assembly

The General Assembly brings together one delegate from each member association. It meets at least once a year to approve the annual report, the accounts and the work plan, and to elect the Board.

## Board

The Board is made up of volunteers elected from member associations. It guides the federation between General Assemblies and represents FEDERG externally.

| Role | Term |
| --- | --- |
| President | 3 years |
| Vice-president | 3 years |
| Secretary | 3 years |
| Treasurer | 3 years |
