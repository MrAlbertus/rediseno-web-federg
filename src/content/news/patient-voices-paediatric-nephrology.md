At this year's European paediatric nephrology meeting, FEDERG co-organised a session in which parents and young adults spoke directly to clinicians.

Topics included the move from children's to adult services, school life and the emotional load of long-term treatment.

> "Doctors know the disease. Families know the life around it. We need both." – a fictional parent quoted for this sample article.
